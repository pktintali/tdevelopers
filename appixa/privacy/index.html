Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Welcome to Appixa, an AI-powered photo editing application.

Your privacy is important to us, and this Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use the Appixa mobile application ("App", "Service").

By using Appixa, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Appixa is designed with a privacy-first approach. We do not collect or store personal data unnecessarily.

a) Account Sign-In

Appixa allows users to sign in using:

  • Google Sign-In
  • Apple Sign-In

When you sign in, we may receive basic account information from the authentication provider, such as:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Unique account identifier

This information is used only for authentication, account management, and subscription access. We do not sell or share this information.

b) Images and Media

To use the editing features, you may select images from your device.

  • Images are accessed only when you choose them.
  • Images are processed securely to provide AI editing or generation features.
  • We do not permanently store your photos on our servers unless explicitly required for processing.
  • Images are not used for advertising or profiling.

2. How We Use Information

We use limited information solely to:

  • Provide AI photo editing and generation features
  • Authenticate users securely
  • Maintain app functionality and performance
  • Enable subscriptions and usage credits
  • Improve service reliability and security

3. Data Processing and Security

All processing is performed using secure systems and industry-standard security practices.

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including:

  • Secure network communication (HTTPS encryption)
  • Restricted access to processing systems
  • Secure authentication mechanisms

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Information may only be shared with trusted third-party service providers strictly necessary to operate the app, such as:

  • Authentication providers (Google / Apple)
  • Secure cloud infrastructure used for AI processing

These providers process data only to deliver the requested services.

5. Data Retention

  • Account-related information is retained only while your account remains active.
  • Images selected for editing are processed temporarily and are not stored permanently unless required for processing operations.
  • Users may stop using the service at any time.

6. Children's Privacy

Appixa is not intended for children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required by local laws).

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
