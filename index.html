Principles

Built on a few non-negotiables.

01

Quality over quantity

Pixels, copy, performance — every detail gets attention. We ship when it's right, not when it's done.

02

Trust by default

Honest descriptions, fair pricing, real privacy. No dark patterns, no manipulative paywalls — ever.

03

Ship fast, learn faster

Indie velocity, big-studio polish. Iterate on what real users actually want, not what we assume.

04

Made with care

Apps shouldn't feel like software. They should feel like a thoughtful friend in your pocket.

05

Performance matters

Lean code, fast launches, smooth animations. Respect the user's device, data and time.

06

Listen, then build

Reviews aren't noise — they're a roadmap. Every email gets read. Every reply matters.
