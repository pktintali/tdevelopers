Kozzu

Self-Care Companion

Kozzu is a soft little companion who is always there to listen. Tell Kozzu about your day, check in with how you feel, write it down, or take a minute to breathe. Kozzu remembers what matters to you, and grows a little every time you take care of yourself.

  • Talk to Kozzu any time, by chat or voice call
  • A companion who remembers what matters to you
  • Daily mood check-ins, a private journal and insights
  • Breathing, grounding, calm games and sleep sounds

Kozzu is for adults aged 18 and over. Kozzu is a supportive self-care companion, not a therapist, a doctor or a medical service. If you are in crisis, please call your local emergency number or a crisis line right away.
