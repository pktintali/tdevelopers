Privacy Policy

Kozzu: Self-Care Companion (the "App") is made by TDevelopers ("we", "our", "us"), an independent app studio in India.

Kozzu is a place to talk about how you feel, so we treat what you share with care. This policy explains what we collect, why, who helps us process it, how long we keep it, and how you can delete it.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google (or Apple on iPhone), we receive your name, email address and an account ID from that sign-in.
  • Profile. What you tell Kozzu during setup and in Settings: your name, your companion's name, your language, your goals, how you have been feeling, your preferred check-in time, and, only if you choose to share them, your gender and age range.
  • Conversations. The messages you exchange with Kozzu, and the short written summaries made after voice calls.
  • Voice calls. When you start a call, your microphone audio is streamed live to our AI provider so Kozzu can answer you in real time. We do not record or store call audio. A short written summary of the call is saved to your conversation.
  • Memories. Short notes Kozzu keeps from your conversations so it does not forget the people, plans and feelings you mention.
  • Wellbeing entries. Your daily mood check-ins (mood, emotions, what is affecting you and an optional note), your journal entries, and notes you write in the sleep wind-down.
  • Progress. Your companion's growth, your streak and your Journey timeline, so they survive a new phone.
  • Reports. If you report something Kozzu said, the report includes your reason, your optional note and the last few messages (or the call summary).
  • Purchases. If you subscribe to Kozzu Premium, your subscription status and purchase history. Payments are handled by Google Play or the App Store; we never see your card details.
  • Usage and diagnostics. Which features you use (for example, that a check-in was logged and its mood score, or that an exercise was completed), your setup answers, app sessions, crash reports, your device model and system version, and an approximate location (city and country) worked out from your IP address. We never send the text of your messages, journal entries or memories to analytics.

Some things stay on your phone only: your app lock PIN (stored as a one-way hash) and fingerprint or face unlock, which your phone handles itself.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, files, SMS or call history, and we do not use your advertising ID.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To let you talk to Kozzu by chat and voice, with replies that fit your situation.
  • To remember context between conversations, and to show your check-ins, journal and progress.
  • To back up your account so you can sign in on a new phone and pick up where you left off.
  • To provide Kozzu Premium and check whether a subscription is active.
  • To show crisis resources and to keep the service safe from abuse.
  • To understand, in aggregate, which features help and what breaks, so we can improve the App.

Kozzu has no ads. We do not sell, rent or trade your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. AI Processing

Kozzu's replies are generated by Google's Gemini API. To answer you, the App sends your message (or, during a call, your voice) together with the context Kozzu needs: your recent conversation, your name and profile details, and the memories Kozzu keeps. Google processes this content under the Gemini API terms in order to produce Kozzu's response. AI replies can be wrong or unsuitable; you can report any reply from inside the App.

4. Who Processes Your Data

We use a small number of service providers who process data on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase Authentication: sign-in.
  • Google Gemini API: Kozzu's chat replies, voice calls and call summaries.
  • Amazon Web Services: storage of your account data, in the Mumbai (India) region.
  • Analytics and crash reporting services: to understand app usage and fix crashes.
  • RevenueCat, Google Play and the App Store: subscriptions and purchases.

Some of these providers may process data outside India, for example in the United States, with appropriate safeguards. We may also disclose information if required by law.

5. Storage, Security and Retention

  • All data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS and secure WebSockets).
  • Our servers accept a request only with a valid sign-in token for your account.
  • The server copy of your conversation keeps the last 30 days of messages; older messages are deleted automatically.
  • Your profile, memories, check-ins, journal entries, reports and progress are kept until you delete your account. Journal entries you delete in the App are also deleted from our servers.
  • Our encrypted database backups keep data for up to 35 days, after which it is gone for good.
  • Purchase records are kept by Google Play, Apple and RevenueCat as required for tax and accounting.

6. Deleting Your Data

  • In the App, go to Settings, then Delete account. Your account and your data are deleted right away.
  • No longer have the App? Follow the steps on our account deletion page, or email us from the address you signed in with.
  • You can also clear your conversation or delete journal entries at any time in the App.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in Google Play or in your Apple account settings so you are not charged again.

7. Your Choices and Rights

  • Access and export: Settings, Export my data, gives you a copy of what Kozzu keeps.
  • Correction: edit your profile in Settings at any time.
  • Deletion: as described above.
  • Permissions: the microphone is used only during calls you start, and notifications are optional. You can turn either off in your phone's settings.
  • Questions or requests: write to us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Adults Only

Kozzu is for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at, and may not be used by, anyone under 18. If we learn that we have collected data from someone under 18, we will delete it promptly.

9. Not a Medical Service

Kozzu is a supportive self-care companion. It is not a therapist, a doctor or a medical service, and it does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, please call your local emergency number or a crisis line right away. Kozzu's support screen points you to free helplines, and it is always free to use.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as Kozzu changes. The latest version, with its date, is always on this page and linked from the App's Settings. Continuing to use the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
