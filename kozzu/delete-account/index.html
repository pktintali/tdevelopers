Delete your Kozzu account

This page is for Kozzu: Self-Care Companion, the app by TDevelopers on Google Play. You can delete your account and your data at any time.

Delete from the app (fastest)

  1. Open Kozzu and go to Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm, and sign in once more with Google or Apple when Kozzu asks. This proves the request is really yours.

Your account and your data are deleted right away, and you are signed out.

Delete without the app

If you no longer have the app, email tdeveloperindia@gmail.com from the email address of the Google or Apple account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Kozzu account". We will check that the account is yours, delete it within 30 days, and reply when it is done.

Delete some data, keep your account

  • Your conversation: in Kozzu, open the chat, tap the menu (⋯) at the top and choose Clear chat. The conversation is deleted from the app and from our servers.
  • A journal entry: open the entry, tap its menu and choose Delete. It is deleted from the app and from our servers.
  • Anything else, such as particular memories or check-ins: email tdeveloperindia@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, tell us what to delete, and we will delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your Kozzu sign-in account
  • Your profile: your name, your companion's name, your goals and preferences, and your gender and age range if you shared them
  • Your conversations with Kozzu, including the summaries of your voice calls
  • Everything Kozzu remembers about you
  • Your journal entries and daily mood check-ins
  • Your progress: your companion's growth, your streak and your timeline
  • Any reports you sent about something Kozzu said
Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play (Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions) or in your Apple ID settings, so you are not charged again.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Backups. Deleted data can remain in our encrypted database backups for up to 35 days, after which it is gone for good.
  • Purchase records. Google Play, Apple and our subscription provider (RevenueCat) keep records of purchases as required for tax and accounting. They contain no conversations, check-ins or journal entries.
  • Security logs. Our servers keep short technical logs (an account ID and the time of a request, never what you wrote) to protect the service, and delete them when they are no longer needed for that.
