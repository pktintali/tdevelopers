Privacy Policy

Imiss – Sweet AI Girlfriend ("we", "our", "us") operates the mobile application Imiss (the "App").

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

We collect and store information that you voluntarily provide when using the App, such as:

  • Account Information: User ID, device information, and basic identifiers (no personal identity verification required).
  • Age Range: During onboarding we ask for your age range to confirm that you are 18 or older and to tailor your experience. We store the selected range only — never your date of birth.
  • Chat Messages: Texts, images, and interactions you send or receive within the app.
  • Roleplay and Mood Data: Messages, roleplay selections, and mood preferences used to personalize your experience.
  • Usage Data: App activity, screen interactions, crash logs, and device performance data.
  • Optional Media: Images you upload or share in chat (stored securely on encrypted servers).

We do not collect personally identifiable information such as your real name, address, or contact number unless you explicitly provide it.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to:

  • Provide and improve AI chat, roleplay, and emotional companion experiences.
  • Personalize conversations and mood-based interactions.
  • Maintain your chat and roleplay history.
  • Improve app performance, stability, and features.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information to any third party.

3. Data Storage and Security

  • All chat and roleplay data is stored securely in encrypted cloud databases (AWS DynamoDB and S3).
  • Uploaded images are kept in secure, non-public storage with restricted access.
  • Data older than a defined duration may be automatically deleted to ensure privacy.
  • We use standard security protocols (HTTPS/TLS) for data transmission.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Your messages and interactions are retained only as long as needed for the intended app functions.
  • You may clear your chat or roleplay history anytime within the app settings.
  • Upon uninstalling the app or upon your request, we may delete or anonymize your stored data.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services to enhance your app experience (e.g., analytics, AI models, or crash reporting).

These services may collect limited, non-identifiable information to help us improve the app.

All third-party providers comply with industry-standard data protection practices.

6. Children's Privacy

This app is intended for adults aged 18 and above. It is not directed at, and may not be used by, anyone under 18.

We ask users to confirm their age during onboarding, and users who indicate they are under 18 are not permitted to proceed. We do not knowingly collect or store data from anyone under 18 years of age.

If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected data from such users, we will delete it promptly.

7. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Request access to or deletion of your chat or roleplay data.
  • Control optional permissions such as notifications, camera, or storage access.
  • Opt out of analytics or crash reporting through your device settings.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

The updated version will be available within the App and on the app store listing.

Continued use of the App after updates means you accept the revised policy.

All conversational content generated by the AI is fictional and for entertainment only, and the developer is not responsible for how the user interprets or uses such content.
