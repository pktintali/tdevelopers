Privacy Policy

Introduction

Welcome to Tattoi ("we," "our," or "us").

Tattoi is an AI-powered tattoo design application that helps users generate tattoo concepts, create custom lettering designs, preview tattoo placements on reference images, generate tattoo stencils, and save tattoo ideas.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use the Tattoi mobile application and related services.

By using Tattoi, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Authentication provider information (Google Sign-In, Apple Sign-In)
  • User account identifiers

User Content

To provide tattoo design features, we may collect:

  • Text prompts
  • Tattoo design requests
  • Tattoo style selections
  • Tattoo descriptions
  • Saved tattoo projects
  • Generated tattoo images
  • Tattoo placement preferences

Photos and Images

Tattoi may request access to your device photos so you can:

  • Upload reference images
  • Preview tattoo placements on images
  • Save generated tattoo designs
  • Save tattoo mockups and stencils

Photos are used only to provide requested app functionality.

Purchase Information

Subscriptions and purchases are processed through Apple App Store and Google Play.

We do not collect or store your payment card details.

We may receive:

  • Subscription status
  • Purchase confirmation
  • Transaction identifiers
  • Product entitlement information

Device Information

We may automatically collect:

  • Device model
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Language settings
  • Crash reports
  • Diagnostic information

Analytics Information

We may collect information about how users interact with Tattoi, including:

  • Screens viewed
  • Features used
  • Session duration
  • App performance metrics
  • Error reports

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Generate AI tattoo designs
  • Create tattoo lettering and stencil outputs
  • Save and synchronize user projects
  • Manage subscriptions and purchases
  • Improve app functionality
  • Monitor performance and reliability
  • Respond to support requests
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Comply with legal obligations

AI Processing

Tattoi uses third-party AI providers to generate tattoo designs and related content.

When you submit prompts, descriptions, or images for generation features, this information may be securely transmitted to AI service providers solely for the purpose of generating requested results.

We do not use your content to personally identify you.
