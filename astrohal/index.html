Astrohal

AI Astrology & Kundli

Astrohal is a Vedic astrology app for India. Make your janam kundli in seconds, read your daily horoscope, match kundlis for marriage, and ask an AI astrologer about love, career or money, with answers drawn from your own chart.

  • Your kundli in North or South Indian style
  • Daily horoscope (rashifal)
  • Kundli milan with 36-guna matching
  • Chat with an AI astrologer in English, Hindi and 7 more Indian languages

Astrohal is for adults aged 18 and above. Readings are for entertainment and are not professional advice.
