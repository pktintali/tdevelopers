Privacy Policy

Effective 21 October 2026 · Last updated 13 November 2026

This policy explains what data the Astrohal app ("Astrohal", "the app") collects, why, where it is kept, and how you can delete it. Astrohal is published by TDevelopers ("we", "us"). For any privacy question, write to hello@tdevelopers.in.

In short
  • The birth details of the profiles you add are saved to your account, so they come back when you sign in on a new phone or after reinstalling. Your kundli and chat history stay on your phone; they are sent to our server only to prepare the reply you asked for, and are not stored there.
  • Our server keeps your account, birth profiles, wallet, gems and daily chat minutes, in India (AWS Mumbai).
  • A palm photo you take for a palm reading is used once to write the reading. It is never stored, and neither is the reading.
  • Payments go through Google Play. We never see your card or UPI details.
  • We use Google Analytics to learn which screens and features are used, so we can improve the app. It never receives your name, phone number, birth details or chats.
  • Astrologer replies are written by AI (Google Gemini). Astrohal is for entertainment and is meant for adults (18+).
  • You can delete your account and data at any time from Profile → Delete my account & data, or by asking us.

1. Data we collect and why

DataWhy we use itWhere it is kept
Sign-in details: your mobile number (OTP sign-in) or your Google account's name, email address and profile photo (Google sign-in); a user ID. To create your account, keep you signed in and link your wallet and purchases to you. Google Firebase Authentication, and our server (user ID, phone number or email, name, sign-in method, sign-up date).
Birth details and kundli: name, date, time and place of birth of the profiles you add, and the charts calculated from them. To calculate your kundli, panchang, matching and readings. On your device, and the birth details (not the charts) on our server, linked to your account, so they come back when you sign in again. When you chat, a summary of the chart (the "kundli card") is sent with your message so the astrologer can answer; the kundli card is not stored on our server.
Chat messages with the AI astrologers. To generate replies. On your device. Each message, with the recent conversation and the kundli card, passes through our server to the Google Gemini API, which writes the reply. We do not store chat content on our server and do not log it.
Palm photos: a photo of your palm that you take or choose for a palm reading, and whether it is your left or right hand. To write your AI palm reading. We do not use palm photos to identify you. Sent once from your device through our server to the Google Gemini API, which writes the reading. Neither the photo nor the reading is stored on our server or logged, and the app keeps the photo only in memory while your reading is on screen (to show your hand with the lines marked on it) and deletes the copy the photo picker leaves on your device. If you tap Share, the reading card with your palm photo is shared where you choose. Our server keeps only a count of the palm photos you try each day, which expires after 3 days, and, for a paid reading, the wallet transaction.
Reports of AI replies: if you report a reply, the reply, your question before it, the reason and your language. To review and improve the safety of AI replies. Our server, for up to 180 days.
Wallet and usage: wallet balance and transactions, gems you activate and your jaap count, free and rewarded chat minutes used each day, and chat session timings. To charge chats correctly and show your wallet and gems on any device you sign in on. Our server. Daily minute counters expire after 3 days and chat session timings after 2 days.
Purchases: Google Play order ID, product, amount, purchase token and date; for Astrohal Premium, also its status, renewal date and the chat minutes and palm readings left this period. To confirm the payment with Google and credit your wallet or gem exactly once, and for tax and accounting records. Our server. Payment details (card, UPI, bank) are handled by Google Play and never reach us.
Advertising data: advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, ad interactions. Google AdMob shows banner ads and the optional rewarded ads that unlock extra chat minutes. When you finish a rewarded ad, AdMob tells our server your user ID so the minute is credited. Google AdMob, under Google's advertising policies.
App integrity signals from Google Play Integrity (through Firebase App Check). To check that requests come from the genuine Astrohal app, which protects your wallet from fraud. Google. Our server receives only a pass or fail token.
App usage analytics: screens you open and actions such as starting a chat, sending a message (not its text), buying or activating a gem, and chat timings and amounts spent; your app language, Moon sign, how you signed in and how many profiles you have; device model, OS version, app version and approximate country or region; and an analytics ID tied to your user ID. To understand how Astrohal is used and where people get stuck, so we can fix and improve it. It is not used for advertising, and it does not read your advertising ID. Google Analytics for Firebase, for up to 14 months.
Crash reports: when the app crashes or hits an error, the error and where in the app it happened, device model, OS version, app version and a crash ID. To find and fix bugs, such as a payment screen that gets stuck. Firebase Crashlytics (Google), for up to 90 days. They contain no chat content, birth details, name or phone number.
Technical logs: time, request type, error codes and AI token counts. To keep the service running and fix problems. Our server, for 30 days. They contain no chat content or birth details.

Horoscopes are prepared for a zodiac sign, period and language, not for you, so asking for one sends no personal data.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use your birth details or chats for advertising.

2. AI-generated content

The astrologers in Astrohal are AI personas. Their replies are written by Google's Gemini model from your messages and your chart summary, and palm readings are written by Gemini from your palm photo. We use Gemini as a paid service: under Google's terms for that service, your messages and photos are not used to train Google's models. Google may keep them briefly to detect abuse. AI replies can be wrong. Astrohal is for entertainment and guidance only, and is not a substitute for medical, legal, financial or other professional advice.

3. Who we share data with

We share data only with the service providers that run Astrohal for us, and only as described above:

  • Google: Firebase Authentication (sign-in), Google Analytics for Firebase (app usage), Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports), Gemini API (AI replies and palm readings), Google Play (payments and app integrity) and AdMob (ads).
  • Amazon Web Services: our server and database, in the Mumbai (ap-south-1) region.

Google may process data outside India under its own privacy terms. We may also disclose data when Indian law requires it.

4. How long we keep data

  • Account, birth profiles, wallet and gems: until you delete your account. A profile you delete in the app is deleted from our server too.
  • Purchase records: for as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires. When you delete your account, these records are kept without your user ID, phone number, email or name.
  • Reports of AI replies: up to 180 days, or until you delete your account.
  • Daily minute counters, palm reading counters and chat session timings: 2 to 3 days.
  • Palm photos and palm readings: not kept. The photo is used once to write the reading.
  • Technical logs: 30 days.
  • Crash reports: up to 90 days.
  • App usage analytics: up to 14 months. Deleting your account also resets the analytics ID on your device.
  • Data on your device: until you delete it in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app.

5. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can:

  • ask what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct or update it (you can edit your profiles in the app);
  • withdraw your consent and have your data erased (see Delete your account);
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights for you;
  • raise a grievance with us, and then with the Data Protection Board of India if you are not satisfied.

Write to hello@tdevelopers.in from the email address you sign in with, or include the mobile number you sign in with. We will reply within 7 days.

7. Security

All traffic between the app and our server is encrypted (HTTPS). Server data is encrypted at rest. Every request must carry a valid sign-in token, and payments are confirmed with Google before anything is credited. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

8. Children

Astrohal is meant for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the app, write to us and we will delete the account.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
